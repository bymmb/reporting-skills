# Examples

## Monthly management report

> Prepare a monthly sales and gross-profit report. Confirm definitions and coverage, reconcile totals, and show the largest product contributions.

## Higher revenue and lower profit

> Explain why revenue increased but gross profit fell. Separate volume, mix, price, and unit-cost effects where supported.

## Partial month

> Sales are only available through the 18th. Compare with the equivalent prior cutoff if supported; explain coverage limitations.

## Mixed currencies

> Keep USD and EUR separate. We have not supplied conversion rates.
