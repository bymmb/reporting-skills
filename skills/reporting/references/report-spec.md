# Report specification

Adapt format, language, and length to the request.

1. Decision summary: what changed, supported contributors, and implication.
2. KPI comparison: revenue, COGS, GP, weighted margin, absolute change, valid growth, and margin movement in points; keep currencies separate.
3. Drivers: ranked product contributions and reconciled arithmetic bridge only where appropriate.
4. Next steps: supported checks and labeled hypotheses; do not invent owners or deadlines.
5. Basis: definitions, source cutoff/completeness, exclusions, returns, FX, and material limitations.
6. Evidence ledger: source file/sheet/range or query, filters, calculation, unrounded result, and limitations for headline claims.

Use host spreadsheet/document/PDF workflows for those formats. Verify calculation and rendered output where applicable. Never send or publish a business report without authorization.
